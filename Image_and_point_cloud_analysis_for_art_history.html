Image and point cloud analysis for art history

    Supervisor: Bloch Isabelle
    Contact: isabelle.bloch@sorbonne-universite.fr

    Description:

    Supervision: Isabelle Bloch (LIP6), Dominique Béréziat (LIP6), researchers at Plemo3D platform

    Students: this project can be adapted for 2 to 4 students

    Description: The Plemo3D platform of Sorbonne Université is involved in the acquisition and analysis of historical objects, at various scales (from small coins to large buildings), to suppose studies in art history. The acquired data can be either 3D point clouds or color images.
    The aim of this project is to develop computational methods to help analyzing these data.
    The project can take several directions, depending on the interest of the students and the availability of data. Examples include:
    - automated classification of archeological archaeological ceramics
    - digital reconstruction of archeological objects from shards, based on contour or surface matching
    - automated recognition of archeological sites from high resolution satellite images
    Work:
    1. Literature review and identification of relevant methods to solve one of the above problems.
    2. Development and implementation of a method. Test on real data from the Plemo3D platform.

    Topic split: Yes

    Target students: 3
