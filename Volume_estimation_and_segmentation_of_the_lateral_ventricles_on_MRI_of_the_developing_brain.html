Volume estimation and segmentation of the lateral ventricles on MRI of the developing brain

    Supervisor: Bloch Isabelle
    Contact: isabelle.bloch@sorbonne-universite.fr

    Description:

    Supervision: Isabelle Bloch (LIP6) and Sarah Stricker (neurosurgeon, Zurich)

    Students: this project can be adapted for 2 students

    Description:
    Ventricular dilatation in infants may require surgical intervention to prevent secondary damage to the developing brain. Monitoring ventricular volume with MRI could improve these critical clinical decisions, but surgical risk stratification requires reference values for lateral ventricular volumes in the neonatal period. Computational imaging has advanced neuroimaging considerably. However, automated segmentation of paediatric images remains less developed than in adults because of high anatomical variability at early ages and the small size of paediatric cohorts. In neonates, ventricular segmentation has been performed with U-Net models, and generative models have been used to synthesize MRI scans simulating disease.
    The aim of this project is to develop computational methods to address these problems.
    The project can take two different directions:
    - Detect the inter-hemispheric plane in MRI images, and compare the left and right ventricules, compute their volumes, and analyze potential asymmetries.
    - Evaluate whether synthetic data (generated using diffusion models, CINeMA, etc.) improve deep learning–based ventricular segmentation. The synthetic data of neurotypical cases could further be used to produce an educational video illustrating neurotypical ventricular growth during early brain development.
    Work:
    1. Literature review and identification of relevant methods to solve one of the above problems.
    2. Development and implementation of a method. Test on real data.
